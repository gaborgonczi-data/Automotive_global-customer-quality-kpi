Global Customer Quality KPI Automation (Power BI)
🎯 Executive Summary
Standardized and automated customer complaint reporting across 7 international manufacturing plants. Replaced manual, error-prone Excel processes with a real-time Power BI dashboard, reclaiming critical engineering hours for high-priority quality protection and containment actions.

🚀 The Hybrid Methodology (AI + Human Insight)
This project utilized a hybrid analytical approach:

AI-Assisted Logic & Validation: Gemini (AI) was used to validate complex DAX measures and optimize data transformation logic within Power Query.

Human-in-the-Loop Design: The dashboard architecture, UX design, and KPI strategy were manually curated to ensure they directly address the operational needs of quality engineers.

🛠 Technical Implementation
Tooling: Power BI (DAX, Power Query), SharePoint Online.

Key Technical Achievements:

Standardized DAX: Developed consistent workday-based response time tracking (D1-D6) across all 7 plants to eliminate reporting bias.

Root Cause Analysis: Implemented an automated "Top 3 Recurrent Issues" visual for immediate focus.

Integrity System: Created a "self-auditing" layer where missing data entries are flagged in real-time, forcing data compliance at the source.

📈 Business Impact
Efficiency: Reclaimed significant engineering time (multiple workdays/month globally) by eliminating manual Excel compilation.

Risk Mitigation: Accelerated response time to OEM complaints, preventing high-cost third-party containment and sorting at customer sites.

Data Integrity: Eliminated "data cosmetics" and human error, providing management with 100% transparent, real-time quality metrics.
